# Note 37: Sharing tmpdrop links safely

A tmpdrop link is a key to a file. Share it only with the people who should have it.

## Do not post links in public places

Avoid posting a tmpdrop link on a public forum, a public chat, or social media. Anyone who finds it can download the file before it expires, and you may never know.

## Prefer direct messages

Send the link in a private message or email to the intended recipient. If a group needs it, use a group channel that only those people can see.

## Think about what is inside

- Do not share identity documents, passwords, or private photos through a temporary link unless you have no safer option and you understand the risk.
- For sensitive work files, check whether your organisation provides a controlled sharing tool instead.
- Remove personal details from a file before sharing it, such as tracked changes or hidden metadata, when that matters.

## After sharing

Once the recipient confirms they have the file, you do not need to do anything else: the link will expire on its own. If you shared it with the wrong person by mistake, tell the recipient, re-upload a fresh copy if needed, and choose a shorter expiry next time.
