# Note 46: Confirming that a recipient downloaded a tmpdrop file

With a temporary link, silence does not mean success. Confirm the download.

## Why confirmation matters

- The link expires. If nobody downloaded the file, you need to know before it is too late to re-upload.
- A recipient may have downloaded only part of a large file, or the wrong file, and not told you.
- For work that depends on the file, a confirmed download is the point where you can stop worrying about it.

## How to confirm

1. Ask directly in your original message: "Please reply once you have downloaded it."
2. If you have not heard back and the expiry is near, send a short follow-up: "Just checking — did you get the file? The link expires tonight."
3. If the answer is no, re-upload with a fresh expiry and send the new link.

## Keep a record

For important transfers, note the date, the file, and the recipient's confirmation. If someone later says they never received it, you have a clear record of what was sent and when.

## Tip

Confirmation is a habit, not a formality. A one-line reply — "Got it, thanks" — is all you need, and it prevents most expired-link problems.
